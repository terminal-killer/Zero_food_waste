# Zero Food Waste / AnnaSetu Food Safety & Redistribution Standards

## 1. 4-Hour Time & Temperature Rule
- Cooked perishable food (rice, curries, lentils, gravies) held at ambient room temperature (20C - 35C) is safe for redistribution only within **4 hours** from cooking time.
- If kept continuously hot above **60°C** in insulated catering cambros/hot boxes, the redistribution window extends up to **6 hours**.
- If chilled rapidly and held below **5°C**, redistribution window extends up to **24 hours**.

## 2. Automatic REJECT Criteria (Zero-Tolerance)
1. **Open Plate Leftovers:** Food served onto guest plates or touched by consumer cutlery is strictly REJECTED.
2. **Expired / Past Safe Window:** Any food older than 4 hours without verified hot/cold chain is REJECTED.
3. **Dairy & Rice Without Storage Note:** High-risk foods (kheeri, paneer gravy, curd rice) held ambient with unknown prep time or beyond 3.5 hours are REJECTED.
4. **Sensory Spoilage Signs:** Off-odors, surface foaming, souring, or broken container seals.

## 3. Verdict Categories
- **SAFE:** More than 90 minutes remaining before safe cutoff. Normal priority dispatch.
- **SAFE_IF_FAST:** Between 30 and 90 minutes remaining. Express volunteer dispatch required. Must be delivered and consumed within the hour.
- **REJECT:** Under 30 minutes, or violates any contamination/temperature rule. Pipeline aborts redistribution immediately to prevent foodborne illness.

## 4. Mandatory Disclaimer
*Final sensory inspection and acceptance judgment always rests with the designated receiving welfare organization upon delivery.*
